# FullHouse: a short guide

For owners, admins and staff. The same steps are in the app under **Help**.

## Getting around

- **Today**: what needs attention: low stock, recommendations waiting, the day's figures (owners and admins).
- **Inventory**: stock on hand, deliveries, counts, and each item's history.
- **Schedule & staff**: the team and the published schedule.
- **Recommendations**: changes the Assistant Manager suggests. Nothing happens until someone approves.
- **History & undo**: every change, who made it, and a way to undo it.

On a phone, these are the buttons along the bottom; everything else is under **More**.

## Everyday jobs

**Log a delivery.** Inventory → find the item → **Record change** → **Delivery received** → enter the amount →
**Save change**. For items bought by the case, switch to cases and enter the number of cases. If the connection
drops, press Save again: it won't be counted twice.

**Record what was used or wasted.** Same place, choose **Used** or **Wasted**.

**Count stock.** **Record change** → **Stock count** → enter what's really there. The difference is kept in the
item's history.

**Add an item.** Inventory → **Add item**. Set how it's counted (each, lb, L…), its reorder point, and, if you buy
it by the case or box, how many come in one.

**Bring in your stock list** (owners and admins). Save your spreadsheet as CSV → Inventory → **Import**. Check the
preview: problems are listed by line. Nothing is saved until every line is right.

**The schedule.** Owners and admins add shifts as drafts, then **Publish**. Staff see only published shifts. A shift
that breaks the restaurant's rules (too long, too little rest, too many hours in the week) is refused with the
reason.

**Recommendations** (owners and admins). Each says what it would change and why. **Approve** carries it out;
**Reject** drops it. To ask the Assistant Manager something, press **New task**, e.g. "Check stock and reorder
anything low".

**Undo a mistake** (owners and admins). History & undo → find the change → **Undo**. If something has changed
since, you'll be asked before it's undone anyway.

**Invite someone** (owners and admins). Profile → Team access → make an invite link and send it. It works once and
expires after a week.

## When something's wrong

- **Offline**: a red bar says so. You can look around; saving needs a connection.
- **The AI is unavailable**: everything else still works (stock, the schedule, the menu, history).
- **Signed out unexpectedly**: sign in again. Anything already saved is kept.
- **Something looks wrong, or you're stuck**: press **Send feedback** (in the sidebar, or on the Help page) and say
  what happened. It goes to the people running FullHouse, with the page you were on.

## Your data

Owners and admins can download everything the restaurant keeps in FullHouse, any time: Help → **Download all data**.
It's a zip of spreadsheet files, one per kind of record.
